| Or

The vertical bar (|) means "or". The regular expression finds the pattern on the left side or the pattern on the right side.

/cat|dog/

A cat, a dog and a bird.

The regular expression finds "cat" or "dog".

Or in a group

Put the vertical bar in a group to make the "or" apply to only a part of the pattern.

/([0-9]|[A-Z]){4}/i

1: This text starts and ends 1

The group finds one digit or one letter. {4} finds the group four times, so each match has four characters. The i flag makes [A-Z] also find lowercase letters.

  • "This", "text" and "ends" have four letters, so they are matches.
  • "starts" has six letters. The first four letters are a match. The last two letters are not a match, because a match must have four characters.
  • "and" has only three letters, so it is not a match.

More about or

  • When the two sides are single characters, a character class is shorter: [0-9A-Z] is the same as [0-9]|[A-Z]. See Character class.
