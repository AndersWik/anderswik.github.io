Colgroup

<colgroup> and <col> let you style whole columns, instead of each cell. They come first in the table, after the <caption>:

<table>
  <colgroup>
    <col style="width: 40%">
    <col span="2" style="background-color: #f3f3f3">
  </colgroup>
  <tr>
    <th>Product</th>
    <th>Price</th>
    <th>Stock</th>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>Apples</td>
    <td>25 kr</td>
    <td>40</td>
  </tr>
</table>

span="2" makes one <col> count for two columns.

Good to know

Only a few CSS properties work on columns: width, background-color, border and visibility. A column can't change the text color or the font of its cells. For that, use a class on the cells, or td:nth-child(2) in CSS.
