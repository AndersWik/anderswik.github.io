Video

<video> plays a video. controls shows the play button, the time line and the volume:

<video src="movie.mp4" controls width="640" height="360"></video>

Several formats

Give the video in several formats with <source>. The browser plays the first one it can. Text between the tags is shown by browsers that can't play video at all:

<video controls width="640" height="360" poster="movie.jpg">
  <source src="movie.webm" type="video/webm">
  <source src="movie.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  Your browser can't play this video.
</video>

poster is an image that is shown before the video starts.

Autoplay

Browsers only start a video by itself if it has no sound. For a background video:

<video src="waves.mp4" autoplay muted loop playsinline></video>

playsinline stops phones from opening the video in full screen.

Subtitles

<video src="movie.mp4" controls>
  <track src="movie.sv.vtt" kind="subtitles" srclang="sv" label="Svenska">
</video>
