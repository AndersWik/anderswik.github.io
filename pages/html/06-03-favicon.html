Favicon

A favicon is the small icon in the browser tab and in bookmarks. Add it with <link> in the <head>:

<head>
  <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
  <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.png" type="image/png">
  <link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">
</head>

Formats

  • SVG looks sharp at every size, and can even change color in dark mode.
  • PNG, 32×32 pixels or larger, works everywhere.
  • apple-touch-icon, a 180×180 PNG, is used when someone adds the page to the home screen of an iPhone or iPad.

If there is no <link rel="icon">, browsers look for /favicon.ico at the root of the site.

Installable apps

An app that can be installed also needs icons in its manifest, in more sizes. See the Progressive web apps notes.
