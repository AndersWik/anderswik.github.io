Viewport

Without the viewport tag, a phone shows the page as if the screen were about 980 pixels wide, and makes everything small. Add this line to the <head>, and the page uses the real width of the screen:

<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">
  • width=device-width makes the page as wide as the screen.
  • initial-scale=1 shows it without zoom.

Don't stop zoom

Some pages add maximum-scale=1 or user-scalable=no. Don't: people who need larger text can't zoom then.

Responsive pages

The viewport tag is the HTML part of a page that works on all screens. The rest is CSS, with media queries and flexible layouts, and images with srcset.
