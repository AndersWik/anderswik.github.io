Form elements

Label

Every field needs a <label>. Click on the label and the field gets focus, and screen readers read the label with the field. Connect them with for and id, or put the field inside the label:

<label for="email">Email</label>
<input type="email" id="email" name="email">

<label><input type="checkbox" name="news"> Send me news</label>

Select

A list to choose from:

<label for="size">Size</label>
<select id="size" name="size">
  <option value="s">Small</option>
  <option value="m" selected>Medium</option>
  <option value="l">Large</option>
</select>

Textarea

A field for text with several lines:

<label for="message">Message</label>
<textarea id="message" name="message" rows="5"></textarea>

Fieldset

<fieldset> groups fields that belong together, and <legend> is its title. It is good for a group of radio buttons:

<fieldset>
  <legend>Delivery</legend>
  <label><input type="radio" name="delivery" value="post"> Post</label>
  <label><input type="radio" name="delivery" value="pickup"> Pick up</label>
</fieldset>

Datalist

Suggestions for a text field. The user can still type something else:

<input list="fruits" name="fruit">
<datalist id="fruits">
  <option value="Apple">
  <option value="Banana">
</datalist>
