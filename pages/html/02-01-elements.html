Elements

An element is a piece of a page, like a heading, a paragraph or an image. Most elements have a start tag, some content and an end tag:

<p>This is a paragraph.</p>

<p> is the start tag, </p> the end tag with a slash, and the text between is the content.

Nesting

Elements can be inside other elements. An element that is opened inside another must also be closed inside it:

<p>This is <strong>important</strong> text.</p>   <!-- right -->
<p>This is <strong>important</p></strong>          <!-- wrong -->

Empty elements

Some elements have no content and no end tag. They are called void elements:

<br>
<hr>
<img src="cat.jpg" alt="A cat">
<input type="text">

You may see them written as <br />. The slash is allowed, but not needed in HTML.

Upper or lower case

Tag names are not case-sensitive: <P> and <p> are the same. Lower case is the common way to write them.
