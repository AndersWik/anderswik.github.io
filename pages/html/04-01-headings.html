Headings

There are six levels of headings, from <h1>, the most important, to <h6>, the least:

<h1>Recipes</h1>
<h2>Cakes</h2>
<h3>Chocolate cake</h3>
<h3>Carrot cake</h3>
<h2>Bread</h2>

The outline of the page

Headings make an outline, like a table of contents. Screen readers use it to jump between the parts of a page, and search engines use it to understand what the page is about.

  • Use one <h1> per page, the title of the page.
  • Don't skip levels: an <h4> should come after an <h3>, not directly after an <h2>.
  • Choose the level from the outline, not the size. If you want a smaller heading, change the size with CSS.
