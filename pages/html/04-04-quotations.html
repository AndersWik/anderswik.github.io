Quotations

Long quotes

<blockquote> is a quote from another source, as its own block. cite is the address of the source.

<blockquote cite="https://www.w3.org/">
  <p>The web is for everyone.</p>
</blockquote>

Short quotes

<q> is a short quote inside a line of text. The browser adds the quotation marks.

<p>She said <q>see you tomorrow</q> and left.</p>

Other elements

<p><cite>The Hobbit</cite> was written by J. R. R. Tolkien.</p>
<p>The <abbr title="World Health Organization">WHO</abbr> was founded in 1948.</p>
<address>
  Anders Wik<br>
  Stockholm
</address>
  • <cite> is the title of a work: a book, film or song.
  • <abbr> is an abbreviation. title is shown when you point at it.
  • <address> is contact information for the page or an article.
