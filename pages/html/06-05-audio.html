Audio

<audio> plays sound. Without controls, nothing is shown:

<audio src="song.mp3" controls></audio>

Several formats

<audio controls>
  <source src="song.ogg" type="audio/ogg">
  <source src="song.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
  Your browser can't play this sound.
</audio>

MP3 works in all modern browsers, so one MP3 file is often enough.

Attributes

  • controls shows play, pause, time and volume.
  • loop starts again at the end.
  • preload="none" waits with downloading until you press play.
  • autoplay is blocked by browsers until the user has clicked on the page.
