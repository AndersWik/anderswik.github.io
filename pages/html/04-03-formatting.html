Formatting

These elements mark a part of a text. Most of them say what the text is, and the browser shows it in a matching style.

<p>This is <strong>important</strong> and this is <em>stressed</em>.</p>
<p>Search results <mark>marked</mark> in yellow.</p>
<p>Old price <del>199 kr</del>, new price <ins>149 kr</ins>.</p>
<p>H<sub>2</sub>O and E = mc<sup>2</sup></p>
<p><small>The small print.</small></p>
<p>Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>C</kbd> to copy the <code>&lt;p&gt;</code> code.</p>

Strong or b, em or i?

  • <strong> means important. <b> is only bold, without meaning.
  • <em> means stressed, as when you say a word louder. <i> is only italic, for things like a book title or a word in another language.

Screen readers can say <strong> and <em> differently, so use them when the meaning is there. If you only want the look, use CSS.

The elements

  • <strong> important, <b> bold
  • <em> stressed, <i> italic
  • <mark> highlighted
  • <small> side comments and small print
  • <del> removed text, <ins> added text
  • <sub> subscript, <sup> superscript
  • <code> code, <kbd> a key on the keyboard
