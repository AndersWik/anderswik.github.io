Title page

Set the title, author and date in the preamble and print them with \maketitle.

Title

\title{My report}
\author{Anders Wik}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

Several authors

\author{Anders Wik \and Jane Doe}
\author{Anders Wik\thanks{University of Somewhere} \\ \texttt{about@example.com}}

No date

\date{}
\date{8 November 2026}

Abstract

\begin{abstract}
A short summary of the document.
\end{abstract}

Custom title page

The titlepage environment gives you an empty page to design yourself.

\begin{titlepage}
  \centering
  \vspace*{4cm}
  {\Huge\bfseries My report\par}
  \vspace{1cm}
  {\Large Anders Wik\par}
  \vfill
  {\large \today\par}
\end{titlepage}

More about title pages

  • In article the title is on the first page. Add the class option titlepage to put it on its own page
  • Change the font size with commands like \Huge
