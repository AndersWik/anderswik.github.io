Sections

Sections split the document into parts. They are numbered automatically and added to the table of contents.

Section levels

\part{Part}                 % report and book
\chapter{Chapter}           % report and book
\section{Section}
\subsection{Subsection}
\subsubsection{Subsubsection}
\paragraph{Paragraph}
\subparagraph{Subparagraph}

Without number

A * removes the number and leaves the section out of the table of contents.

\section*{Acknowledgements}

Short title

The text in square brackets is used in the table of contents and page header.

\section[Short title]{A very long title for this section}

Appendix

After \appendix, sections are numbered A, B, C.

\appendix
\section{Extra data}

Numbering depth

\setcounter{secnumdepth}{2}   % number down to subsection

More about sections

  • Refer to a section with \label and \ref, see cross references
  • \chapter does not exist in article
  • The titlesec package changes how headings look
