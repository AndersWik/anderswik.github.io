Headers and footers

The fancyhdr package puts your own text in the header and footer. Each has a left, center and right part.

Header and footer

\usepackage{fancyhdr}
\pagestyle{fancy}
\fancyhf{}                     % clear everything

\fancyhead[L]{My report}
\fancyhead[R]{\leftmark}       % current section
\fancyfoot[C]{\thepage}        % page number
\renewcommand{\headrulewidth}{0.4pt}
\renewcommand{\footrulewidth}{0pt}

Page X of Y

\usepackage{lastpage}
\fancyfoot[C]{Page \thepage\ of \pageref{LastPage}}

Left and right pages

With the twoside option, E is for even pages and O for odd.

\fancyhead[LE,RO]{\thepage}
\fancyhead[RE,LO]{My book}
