Equations

The amsmath package has environments for numbered equations and equations over several lines.

Numbered equation

\usepackage{amsmath}

\begin{equation}
  E = mc^2
  \label{eq:energy}
\end{equation}

See equation \eqref{eq:energy}.

Without number

Add * to any of the environments, or use \nonumber on one line.

\begin{equation*}
  E = mc^2
\end{equation*}

Align

& marks where the lines are aligned and \\ ends a line.

\begin{align}
  f(x) &= (x + 1)^2 \\
       &= x^2 + 2x + 1 \nonumber
\end{align}

Gather

Several centered equations without alignment.

\begin{gather}
  a = b + c \\
  d = e + f
\end{gather}

Long equation

\begin{multline}
  a + b + c + d + e + f \\
  + g + h + i + j
\end{multline}

Cases

\[
  |x| =
  \begin{cases}
    x  & \text{if } x \ge 0 \\
    -x & \text{if } x < 0
  \end{cases}
\]

Custom tag

\begin{equation}
  a = b \tag{*}
\end{equation}

More about equations
