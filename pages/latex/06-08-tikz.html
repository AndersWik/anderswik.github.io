TikZ

TikZ draws figures and diagrams directly in LaTeX. Coordinates are in centimeters by default.

Lines and shapes

\usepackage{tikz}

\begin{tikzpicture}
  \draw (0,0) -- (2,0) -- (2,1);
  \draw[->] (0,0) -- (0,2);
  \draw (3,0) rectangle (4,1);
  \draw (5,0.5) circle (0.5);
  \draw[red, thick, dashed] (0,0) -- (4,2);
  \fill[blue!20] (6,0) rectangle (7,1);
\end{tikzpicture}

Nodes

Nodes are text boxes that can be connected.

\begin{tikzpicture}
  \node[draw] (a) at (0,0) {Write};
  \node[draw] (b) at (3,0) {Commit};
  \node[draw, circle] (c) at (6,0) {Push};
  \draw[->] (a) -- (b);
  \draw[->] (b) -- (c);
\end{tikzpicture}

Plot a function

\usepackage{pgfplots}
\pgfplotsset{compat=1.18}

\begin{tikzpicture}
  \begin{axis}[xlabel=$x$, ylabel=$y$]
    \addplot[domain=-2:2, blue] {x^2};
  \end{axis}
\end{tikzpicture}
