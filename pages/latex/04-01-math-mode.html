Math mode

Math is written in math mode. Letters become italic variables and spaces are ignored, LaTeX sets the spacing itself.

Inline math

The area is $\pi r^2$.
The area is \(\pi r^2\).

Display math

Display math is centered on its own line. $$ ... $$ also works but should be avoided.

\[
  E = mc^2
\]

Subscript and superscript

Use braces when there is more than one character.

x^2   x_i   x_i^2   x^{10}   x_{i,j}   e^{-x^2}

Fractions and roots

\frac{a}{b}
\dfrac{a}{b}      % large fraction, amsmath
\tfrac{a}{b}      % small fraction, amsmath
\sqrt{x}
\sqrt[3]{x}

Text in math

\[ f(x) = 0 \quad \text{for all } x \]

Brackets

\left and \right grow with the content.

\left( \frac{a}{b} \right)
\left[ x \right]
\left\{ x \right\}
\left| x \right|
\langle x \rangle

Spacing

a\,b    a\:b    a\;b    a\quad b    a\qquad b    a\!b
