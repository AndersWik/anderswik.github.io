Tables

Tables are made with the tabular environment. The column types are set after it, & splits the columns and \\ ends a row.

Table

\begin{tabular}{l c r}
  Left & Center & Right \\
  a    & b      & c     \\
\end{tabular}

Column types

l           % left
c           % center
r           % right
p{4cm}      % paragraph with a fixed width, text wraps
|           % vertical line between columns

Lines

\begin{tabular}{|l|r|}
  \hline
  Name  & Price \\
  \hline
  Apple & 5     \\
  Pear  & 7     \\
  \hline
\end{tabular}

booktabs

booktabs gives nicer horizontal lines. Skip the vertical lines.

\usepackage{booktabs}

\begin{tabular}{lr}
  \toprule
  Name  & Price \\
  \midrule
  Apple & 5     \\
  Pear  & 7     \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}

Table with caption

Put the tabular inside a table float to add a caption and number.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Fruit prices}
  \label{tab:prices}
  \begin{tabular}{lr}
    \toprule
    Name  & Price \\
    \midrule
    Apple & 5     \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Merge cells

\usepackage{multirow}

\multicolumn{2}{c}{Two columns} \\
\multirow{2}{*}{Two rows} & a \\
                          & b \\

Full width

\usepackage{tabularx}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l X}
  Name & A long text that fills the rest of the line. \\
\end{tabularx}
