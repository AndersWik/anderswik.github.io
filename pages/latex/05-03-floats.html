Floats

Figures and tables are floats. LaTeX moves them to where they fit best, so they do not leave big gaps on the page.

Placement

\begin{figure}[htbp]
h    here, if possible
t    top of a page
b    bottom of a page
p    on a page with only floats
!    try harder
H    exactly here, needs the float package

Exactly here

\usepackage{float}

\begin{figure}[H]

Captions

\caption{A long caption shown under the figure}
\caption[Short]{A long caption}   % short one in the list of figures
\caption*{No number}              % with the caption package

Text around an image

\usepackage{wrapfig}

\begin{wrapfigure}{r}{0.4\textwidth}
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.38\textwidth]{penguin}
  \caption{Tux}
\end{wrapfigure}

More about floats

  • Write \label after \caption, or the reference gets the wrong number
  • \clearpage prints all waiting floats
  • \usepackage[section]{placeins} keeps floats in their own section
