Colors

The xcolor package adds colors to text and backgrounds.

Text color

\usepackage{xcolor}

\textcolor{red}{Red text}
{\color{blue} Blue text}
\textcolor{red!50}{50% red}
\textcolor{blue!60!black}{Dark blue}

Background

\colorbox{yellow}{Highlighted}
\fcolorbox{red}{white}{With a red frame}
\pagecolor{black}

Own colors

\definecolor{myblue}{RGB}{0, 102, 204}
\definecolor{mygreen}{HTML}{2E8B57}
\definecolor{mygray}{gray}{0.6}

Named colors

red green blue cyan magenta yellow black white gray
darkgray lightgray brown lime olive orange pink purple teal violet

\usepackage[dvipsnames]{xcolor}   % 68 more, like ForestGreen and NavyBlue

More about colors

  • \usepackage[table]{xcolor} adds \rowcolor and \cellcolor for tables
  • The soul package has \hl{text} to highlight across lines
