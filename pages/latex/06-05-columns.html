Columns

Text can be set in two or more columns, for the whole document or just a part.

Two columns

\documentclass[twocolumn]{article}

multicol

\usepackage{multicol}

\begin{multicols}{3}
  Text in three columns.
  \columnbreak
  Starts in the next column.
\end{multicols}

Side by side with minipage

minipage makes small boxes that can be placed next to each other.

\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
  Left side.
\end{minipage}
\hfill
\begin{minipage}{0.48\textwidth}
  Right side.
\end{minipage}

More about columns

  • Space and line between columns: \setlength{\columnsep}{1cm} and \setlength{\columnseprule}{0.4pt}
  • A figure over both columns in twocolumn uses figure*
