Alignment

Text is justified by default, so both edges are straight.

Environments

\begin{center}
  Centered text.
\end{center}

\begin{flushleft}
  Left aligned text.
\end{flushleft}

\begin{flushright}
  Right aligned text.
\end{flushright}

Switches

Switches do not add extra space around the text. Use \centering inside figures and tables.

{\centering Centered text.\par}
\raggedright
\raggedleft
