Quotes

LaTeX does not turn " into curly quotation marks. Use two backticks and two apostrophes, or the csquotes package.

Quotation marks

``double quotes''
`single quotes'

csquotes

\enquote uses the right marks for the language set with babel.

\usepackage{csquotes}

\enquote{Talk is cheap. Show me the code.}
\enquote{He said \enquote{hello}.}

Block quotes

\begin{quote}
  A short quote.
\end{quote}

\begin{quotation}
  A longer quote with several paragraphs.
\end{quotation}

\begin{verse}
  Roses are red,\\
  Violets are blue.
\end{verse}
