Math symbols

Most symbols are commands in math mode. Many need amssymb.

\iota \kappa \lambda \mu \nu \xi \pi \rho \sigma \tau \upsilon
\phi \varphi \chi \psi \omega

Relations

=  \neq  <  >  \le  \ge  \ll  \gg  \approx  \equiv  \sim  \propto

Sums, products, integrals and limits

\sum_{i=1}^{n} i
\prod_{i=1}^{n} i
\int_{0}^{\infty} f(x)\,dx
\iint  \iiint  \oint
\lim_{x \to 0} f(x)

Functions

Function names are written upright. Define your own with \DeclareMathOperator.

\sin \cos \tan \log \ln \exp \max \min \det

\DeclareMathOperator{\sgn}{sgn}
