Emphasis

Wrap text in * or _ to make it italic or bold. One for italic, two for bold, three for both.

Italic

*italic*
_italic_

Bold

**bold**
__bold__

Bold and italic

***bold and italic***
**bold with _italic_ inside**

Strikethrough

Two tildes strike the text through. This is part of GitHub Flavored Markdown and works in most editors.

~~no longer true~~

Inside a word

Use * for emphasis in the middle of a word. _ inside a word is usually left as it is, so snake_case_name stays the same.

un*believ*able

Highlight, subscript and superscript

These are not in standard Markdown. Some editors and Pandoc support them, otherwise use HTML.

==highlighted==        <mark>highlighted</mark>
H~2~O                  H<sub>2</sub>O
x^2^                   x<sup>2</sup>
                       <ins>underlined</ins>

More about emphasis

  • No space between the symbol and the text: ** bold ** does not work
  • Pick one style and stick with it, for example *italic* and **bold**
  • To show a literal *, escape it with \*
