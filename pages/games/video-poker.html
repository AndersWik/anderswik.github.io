Video Poker

Jacks or Better

Choose your bet and press Deal.

Bet 1

Credits 100

How to play
  • Choose how many credits to bet, 1 to 5, and press Deal. You get five cards.
  • Click the cards you want to keep, or press the keys 1 to 5. They are marked HELD.
  • Press Draw. The cards you didn't keep are swapped for new ones.
  • Your final hand pays as the table says, times your bet. The lowest win is a pair of jacks or better: a pair of 2s to 10s pays nothing.
  • Your credits are saved. If they run out, you can start over with 100.
