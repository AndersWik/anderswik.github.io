Fia

How to play
  • Each player has four pieces in their base, in their corner. Red starts, and the turn goes clockwise.
  • Coming out: roll a 6 to move a piece out to your start circle, the circle in your color at the end of the arm by your base. With no pieces out, you get three tries to roll a 6.
  • Moving: move one piece as many circles as the die shows, clockwise around the board. If no piece can move, the turn goes on to the next player.
  • Rolling a 6 gives you another roll.
  • One piece per circle: you can never land on your own piece.
  • Knocking out: land on another player's piece, and it goes back to its base.
  • Going home: after one lap, your pieces go in along the middle of the arm where they started, on the four home circles in your color. You can't go past the last one.
  • Winning: the first player with all four pieces in the home circles wins.
