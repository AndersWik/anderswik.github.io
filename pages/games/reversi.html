Reversi

How to build this game

Reversi: the game for two players on one screen. The board, the rules for flipping pieces, the dots that show where you may play, and passing.

Reversi vs computer: carries on from the first tutorial and adds the computer player. The easy computer takes the move that flips the most pieces, the hard one looks four moves ahead.
