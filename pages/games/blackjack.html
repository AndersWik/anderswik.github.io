Blackjack

Dealer

You

Balance: $1000 · Bet: $0
How to play
  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer, without going over. Number cards count their number, J, Q and K count 10, and an ace counts 11 or 1.
  • Betting: click the chips to place a bet, then Deal. You get two cards, and the dealer gets one face up and one face down.
  • Hit takes another card, Stand keeps what you have. Over 21 is bust, and you lose the bet.
  • The dealer turns the hidden card, and draws until 17 or more.
  • Blackjack, 21 with your first two cards, pays 3 to 2. Other wins pay 1 to 1, and a tie gives your bet back.
  • Double down: double your bet on your first two cards, and get exactly one more card.
  • Split: two cards of the same value become two hands, each with the same bet. Once per round.
  • Insurance: when the dealer shows an ace, bet half your bet that the dealer has blackjack. It pays 2 to 1.
