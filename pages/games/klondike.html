Klondike

Moves 0 · 0:00

Drag the cards, or tap a card to move it.

How to play
  • Move all 52 cards to the four piles at the top right, one pile for each suit, from the ace up to the king.
  • In the seven columns, put a card on one that is one higher and the other color: a red 9 on a black 10. You can move a whole run of cards at once.
  • Only a king, or a run that starts with a king, can go in an empty column.
  • When a column's face-up cards are moved away, the card under them turns over.
  • Tap the pile at the top left to turn over 1 or 3 new cards. When it is empty, tap it again to start it over.
  • Tap a card to send it where it fits best: home to its pile if it can, otherwise to a column. Or drag it where you want it.
  • Your game is saved, so you can come back to it later.
