Connect Four

How to build this game

Connect Four: the game for two players on one screen. The board, dropping pieces into a column, and checking for four in a row.

Connect Four vs computer: carries on from the first tutorial and adds the computer player. The easy computer follows three simple rules, the hard one tries every move five moves ahead with minimax.
