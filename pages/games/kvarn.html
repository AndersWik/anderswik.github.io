Kvarn (Nine Men's Morris)

How to play
  • Two players, red and blue, have nine pieces each. Red starts.
  • Placing: take turns placing your pieces, one at a time, on any free point.
  • Mills: three of your pieces in a row along one of the lines is a mill. Make a mill, and you remove one of the other player's pieces. Pieces in a mill can't be removed, unless all their pieces are in mills.
  • Moving: when all pieces are placed, take turns moving one of your pieces along a line to a free point next to it. Moving a piece out of a mill and back in makes a new mill.
  • Jumping: with only three pieces left, you may move a piece to any free point.
  • Winning: the other player loses when they have fewer than three pieces, or can't move.
