Minesweeper

Mines left: 10 Time: 0

How to play
  • Some squares hide a mine. Dig up all the other squares to win.
  • A number tells how many mines there are in the 8 squares around it.
  • Your first dig is always safe, and opens up a bit of the field.
  • Flag a square where you think there is a mine: right-click it, or switch the button above the field from Dig to Flag. A flagged square can't be dug by mistake.
  • Click a number that has as many flags around it as its number, and all the other squares around it are dug at once.
  • Dig up a mine, and the game is over.
