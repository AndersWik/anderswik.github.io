Steady Hand

Hold your phone like a cup of coffee, and tap Start.

How to play
  • Hold the phone in front of you, like a cup of coffee, and keep it as still as you can for 10 seconds.
  • Every little wobble makes the coffee slosh. If it goes over the edge, it spills, and you try again.
  • Every level fills the cup a little higher, so it spills more easily.
  • The phone's gyroscope feels even a tiny tremble. Tip: hold it with both hands, elbows against your body, and breathe out slowly.
  • On a computer, hold the mouse still over the cup instead.
