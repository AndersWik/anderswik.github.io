Ludo

How to play
  • Each player has four pieces in their base, the big square in their corner. Red starts, and the turn goes clockwise.
  • Coming out: roll a 6 to move a piece out to your start square, the square in your color by your base. With no pieces out, you get three tries to roll a 6.
  • Moving: move one piece as many squares as the die shows, clockwise around the board. If no piece can move, the turn goes on to the next player.
  • Rolling a 6 gives you another roll.
  • Knocking out: land on another player's piece, and it goes back to its base. Your own pieces may share a square.
  • Going home: after one lap, your pieces turn into the lane in your color and go up it to the middle. You need the exact number to reach the middle.
  • Winning: the first player with all four pieces in the middle wins.
