Chinese Checkers

How to play
  • You are red, at the bottom. Move all ten of your pieces to the point of the star on the other side. The first player to get there wins.
  • Click a piece, then one of the dots. A piece moves one step to an empty hole next to it, or jumps.
  • A jump goes over one piece next to it, yours or anyone's, to the empty hole right behind. After a jump you may jump again, as many times as you can, in any direction. Nothing is taken.
  • The dots show every hole the piece can reach, also at the end of a long chain of jumps.
  • Tip: build "ladders" of pieces with gaps between them, so one piece can jump far in one move.
