Compression

Compression makes files smaller, and archiving bundles many files into one. On Linux these are often two steps: tar puts files in one archive and gzip or another tool compresses it, which gives files like backup.tar.gz.

Common formats

  • .tar — an archive, not compressed
  • .gz and .tar.gz (.tgz) — gzip compression
  • .bz2, .xz and .zst — other compressions that are often smaller
  • .zip and .7z — archive and compression in one, common on Windows

Zip files

$ zip -r archive.zip folder
$ unzip archive.zip

Commands in this chapter

  • tar — create and extract archives
  • gzip — compress single files
  • 7z — 7-Zip archives
