Services

A service, or daemon, is a program that runs in the background, like a web server, database or SSH server. On most modern distributions services are started and supervised by systemd, which also collects their logs.

Units

systemd calls the things it manages units. Services end in .service and their configuration files are found in /lib/systemd/system/ and /etc/systemd/system/.

$ systemctl list-units --type=service

Commands in this chapter

  • systemctl — start, stop and enable services
  • journalctl — read the logs
