Play patterns together

A song has several parts that play at the same time, like drums, bass and a melody. In Strudel, each part starts with $:. All the parts play together when you press Ctrl + Enter.

$: sound("bd sd")
$: note("c eb g")

Mute a part

Change $: to _$: to mute a part without removing it. Change it back to bring the part back.

$: sound("bd sd")
_$: note("c eb g")

A small song

Here the bassline, a melody and the drums from the other pages play together. Play it, and then mute and change the parts while it plays.

$: note("<[c2 c3]*4 [bb1 bb2]*4 [f2 f3]*4 [eb2 eb3]*4>")
  .sound("gm_synth_bass_1").lpf(800)

$: n(`<
[~ 0] 2 [0 2] [~ 2]
[~ 0] 1 [0 1] [~ 1]
[~ 0] 3 [0 3] [~ 3]
[~ 0] 2 [0 2] [~ 2]
>*4`).scale("C4:minor")
  .sound("gm_synth_strings_1")

$: sound("bd*4, [~ <sd cp>]*2, [~ hh]*4")
  .bank("RolandTR909")

Save your work

The REPL keeps your code in the browser. To keep a pattern safe, copy the code to a file. The share button at the top makes a link with the code in it, so you can send a pattern to someone else.
