Effects

Effects change how a sound sounds. They are functions you add after the sound with a dot. Every effect takes a number, or a pattern of numbers in quotes.

Filter

lpf is a low-pass filter. It removes the high frequencies, so the sound gets darker. The number is the frequency in hertz: a lower number is a darker sound.

note("c2 c3 c2 c3").sound("sawtooth").lpf("<400 2000>")

hpf is a high-pass filter, which does the opposite. vowel is a filter that makes the sound talk:

note("c3 eb3 g3").sound("sawtooth").vowel("<a e i o>")

Volume

gain sets the volume. 1 is normal. A pattern of volumes gives the rhythm some life.

sound("hh*16").gain("[.25 1]*2")

Envelope

adsr shapes each note: attack, decay, sustain and release, written with colons between them.

note("c3 bb2 f3 eb3").sound("sawtooth").lpf(600).adsr(".1:.1:.5:.2")

Delay and reverb

delay adds echoes. room adds reverb, as if the sound is played in a large room. Both take a number from 0 to 1.

sound("bd rim bd cp").delay(.5)
sound("bd rim bd cp").room(.5)

Pan

pan moves the sound between the left (0) and the right (1) speaker.

sound("bd rim bd cp").pan("0 1")

Speed

speed plays a sample faster or slower, which also makes it higher or lower. A negative number plays it backwards.

sound("bd rim bd cp").speed("<1 2 -1 -2>")

Signals

A signal is a value that changes smoothly over time. sine goes up and down between 0 and 1 once a cycle. saw goes up and then jumps back.

sound("hh*16").gain(sine)

range sets the lowest and highest value, and slow makes the signal take more cycles. Here the filter opens and closes over 4 cycles:

note("c2 c3 c2 c3").sound("sawtooth").lpf(sine.range(100, 2000).slow(4))

Slow a whole pattern

slow also works on a whole pattern. fast does the opposite.

sound("bd*4, ~ rim ~ cp").slow(2)
