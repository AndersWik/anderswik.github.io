First sounds

sound plays a sequence of sounds. Write the names of the sounds in a string, with spaces between them. s is a short name for sound.

sound("bd hh sd oh")
s("bd hh sd oh")

Drum sounds

The drum sounds have short names:

  • bd bass drum
  • sd snare drum
  • rim rimshot
  • cp clap
  • hh closed hi-hat
  • oh open hi-hat
  • lt, mt, ht low, middle and high tom
  • cr crash and rd ride

There are also other sounds, like casio, jazz, metal and wind. The sounds tab in the REPL lists them all, and you can click a name to hear it.

sound("casio")

Drum machines

bank chooses which drum machine the drum sounds come from.

sound("bd hh sd oh").bank("RolandTR909")

Try RolandTR808, RolandTR707 or RolandTR505 too. The functions are joined with a dot, so you can add more after each other.

Sample numbers

Most sounds have several samples. Add : and a number to choose one. The first is 0.

sound("casio:1")
sound("jazz:0 jazz:1 jazz:4 jazz:3")

n gives the sample numbers as a pattern of their own, which is shorter:

n("0 1 4 3").sound("jazz")

Tempo

All the steps in a pattern share one cycle. More steps means faster steps, not a longer pattern. The tempo is set in cycles per minute with setcpm. The default is 30, one cycle every 2 seconds.

setcpm(90/4)
sound("bd hh sd hh")

90/4 is a way to think in beats per minute: 90 beats per minute, with 4 beats in a cycle.

Two classic beats

A basic rock beat. The comma plays the two parts at the same time, see Mini-notation.

setcpm(100/4)
sound("[bd sd]*2, hh*8").bank("RolandTR505")

A house beat. - is a rest, a step with no sound.

sound("bd*4, [- cp]*2, [- hh]*4").bank("RolandTR909")
