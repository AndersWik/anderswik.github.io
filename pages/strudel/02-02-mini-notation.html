Mini-notation

The text inside the quotes, like "bd sd", is written in mini-notation: a short language for rhythms. It is the same for sounds, notes and numbers. These are the symbols you use the most.

SymbolMeaningExample
- or ~Rest, a step with no sound"bd - sd -"
[ ]Sub-sequence, several steps in one step"bd [hh hh]"
*Speed up, play a step several times"hh*4"
/Slow down, stretch over several cycles"[c e g b]/2"
< >Alternate, one step per cycle"<bd cp>"
,Play at the same time"bd*2, hh*4"
!Replicate, repeat a step"c!3 e"
@Elongate, make a step longer"c@3 e"
:Sample number"casio:1"

Rests

- is a step with no sound. ~ does the same thing.

sound("bd hh - rim - bd hh rim")

Sub-sequences

Brackets put several steps in the space of one. [hh hh] plays two hi-hats in the time of one step.

sound("bd [hh hh] sd [hh bd] bd - [hh sd] cp")

Sub-sequences can be inside other sub-sequences:

sound("bd [[rim rim] hh] bd cp")

Speed up

* plays a step, or a sub-sequence, several times in its own space.

sound("bd hh*2 rim hh*3 bd [- hh*2] rim hh*2")
sound("bd [hh rim]*2 bd [hh rim]*1.5")

Slow down

/ does the opposite. /4 stretches the sequence over 4 cycles, so you hear one note per cycle.

note("[36 34 41 39]/4").sound("gm_acoustic_bass")

Alternate

Angle brackets play one step per cycle: the first step in the first cycle, the second in the second cycle, and so on. Adding steps doesn't change the speed.

sound("<bd bd hh bd rim bd hh bd>")

Speed it up with * to get a fast pattern you can make as long as you want:

sound("<bd bd hh bd rim bd hh bd>*8")

Inside a pattern, it changes one step in each cycle:

sound("bd*4, [- <sd cp>]*2, [- hh]*4").bank("RolandTR909")

Play at the same time

A comma plays two sequences at the same time. Each sequence keeps its own speed.

sound("hh hh hh, bd casio")

Replicate and elongate

! repeats a step. Unlike *, the repeats take steps of their own.

note("c!3 e").sound("piano")

@ makes a step longer. Here c is three times as long as eb.

note("c@3 eb").sound("piano")

Long patterns

Strings in backticks can go over several lines. This makes long patterns easier to read.

sound(`bd*2, - cp,
- - - oh, hh*4,
[- casio]*2`)
