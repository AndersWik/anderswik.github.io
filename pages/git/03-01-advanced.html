Advanced

The commands in this chapter are used less often, but are very useful once you know the basics. Tags mark important commits such as releases, and rebase lets you clean up the history before you share it.

Commands in this chapter

  • Tagging — give a commit a permanent name, like v1.0
  • Rebase — move, combine and edit commits

Rewriting history

Several commands, such as amend, reset and rebase, change commits that already exist. That is fine for commits only you have. Once commits are pushed and others have them, changing them causes problems for everyone else.
