MySQL Playground

Log in to a pretend MySQL server and try SQL. The password for root is root. Nothing here touches your computer, and reset in the shell puts everything back.

Tab completes names, ↑ and ↓ go through earlier commands, Ctrl+L clears the screen, Ctrl+C cancels a line. man mysql shows the manual (q to quit).
