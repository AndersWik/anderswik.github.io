How to play
  • The cards are dealt in 13 piles of 4: twelve round a clock, with the ace at 1 o'clock, the 2 at 2 o'clock and so on to the queen at 12 o'clock. The kings' pile is in the middle.
  • The top card of the middle pile is turned over. Tap the pile at its hour: a 7 goes to 7 o'clock, a jack to 11 and a queen to 12. A king goes back to the middle.
  • The card goes under that pile, face up, and the top card of that pile is turned over next.
  • The game ends when the fourth king is turned over. You win if every card is face up by then.
  • There are no choices to make: the deal decides if you win. You win about one game in 13.
