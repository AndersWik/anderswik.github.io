Pop and Bump

How to play
  • Each player has four pieces in their base, in their corner. Red starts, and the turn goes clockwise.
  • Rolling: pop the bubble in the middle to roll the die.
  • Coming out: roll a 6 to move a piece out to your start hole, the hole with a ring in your color. With no pieces out, you get three tries to roll a 6.
  • Moving: move one piece as many holes as the die shows, clockwise around the ring. If no piece can move, the turn goes on to the next player.
  • Rolling a 6 gives you another pop.
  • One piece per hole: you can never land on your own piece.
  • Bumping: land on another player's piece, and it goes back to its base.
  • Going home: after one lap, your pieces go into the four home holes in your color, towards the middle. You can't go past the last one.
  • Winning: the first player with all four pieces in the home holes wins.
