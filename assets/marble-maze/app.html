Marble Maze

Hold your phone the way you like, and tap Start.

How to play
  • Tilt the phone to roll the marble through the maze to the green hole.
  • Don't roll into the black holes: the marble falls through and starts again.
  • When you press Start, the way you hold the phone counts as flat. Hold it still for a moment, then tilt from there.
  • Every level is bigger, with more holes. Your best time for each level is saved.
  • On a computer, use the arrow keys to tilt the board.
