Shake Dice

Dice:

Shake the phone to roll, or tap Roll.

How to play
  • Choose the kind of dice, D4 to D20, and how many.
  • Shake the phone, then stop. The harder you shake, the harder the dice are thrown: they fly faster, bounce more and roll longer.
  • The numbers are fair: shaking harder changes the throw, not the odds.
  • The bar under the dice shows how hard you shake, and the white line your hardest shake so far.
  • Hold on to the phone! On a computer, press Roll.
