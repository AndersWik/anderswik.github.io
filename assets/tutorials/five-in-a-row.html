Five in a Row

How to play
  • Two players, black and white, take turns placing a stone on any free point where the lines cross. Black starts.
  • Stones are never moved or taken away.
  • Winning: the first to get five or more stones in a row wins: across, down or diagonally.
  • Blocking: four in a row with both ends free can't be stopped, so block a row of three before it gets there.
  • If the board fills up with no five in a row, it's a draw.
  • The game is also called Gomoku, and in Sweden luffarschack.
