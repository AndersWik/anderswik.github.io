Hi-Lo Counter

What is the count?

    How to play
    • Cards are shown one at a time. Keep a count in your head, with the Hi-Lo values below. When the cards stop, say what the count is.
    • Easy: every round starts at 0, with a new deck.
    • Hard: the count keeps running from round to round, like at a real table. Say the total so far.
    • Card counters use the count in blackjack: a high count means many tens and aces are left, which is good for the player.
