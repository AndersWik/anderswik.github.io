Spin Counter

Hold your phone tight, and tap Start.

How to play
  • Tap Start and hold the phone still for the countdown, so it can find out which way is down.
  • Then spin round on the spot, holding the phone tight in both hands. Or lay it flat on a smooth table and spin it.
  • Spin as many full turns as you can in 10 seconds. Every turn buzzes. Turning back and forth doesn't count, only turns the same way.
  • Your best is saved.
  • Make room first, watch out for people and things around you, and stop if you get dizzy.
  • On a computer, drag round the dial with the mouse instead.
