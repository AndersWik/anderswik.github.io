How to play
  • Every game has a topic, picked at random: animals, space, the human body, food and drink, around the world, science and inventions, the ocean, or sports and games.
  • You get five questions about the topic. Click the answer you think is right.
  • The right answer turns green. If you picked a wrong one, it turns red.
  • Each topic has 50 questions, and you won't get the same question again until you have seen them all.
